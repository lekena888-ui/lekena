About Lesotho

Get to know the country and its people.

A small country with a big view

Lesotho is surrounded by South Africa and is known as the Kingdom in the Sky because of its high elevation. Mountains, valleys and rivers shape much of the landscape, while visitors can learn about Basotho traditions and heritage.

Basotho culture includes the mokorotlo (traditional hat), blankets, music and crafts. Visitors should be respectful when visiting communities and ask permission before photographing people.

A few things to know

Capital city

Maseru is the capital and main city of Lesotho.

Languages

Sesotho and English are widely used in the country.

Landscape

Mountains, valleys, rivers and rural villages shape much of the scenery.

Frequently asked questions

Visitors can enjoy hiking, pony trekking, sightseeing, cultural visits and photography. Arrange local guides where appropriate and check road and weather conditions.

Pack comfortable walking shoes, warm layers and weather-appropriate clothing. Mountain weather can change quickly.
